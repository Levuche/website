# Getting help

Where to go depends on what you need.

- **Questions, ideas and feedback:** start a [discussion](https://github.com/Levuche/website/discussions). Use **Q&A** for questions, **Ideas** for suggestions and **Show and tell** for what you've made with Levuche.
- **Something in this repository is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Levuche/website/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **A security problem:** report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) explains.
- **Your own Levuche account, an order, a refund or a payout:** contact us through [levuche.com/contact](https://levuche.com/contact). The team can look at your account there; on GitHub we can't, and everything is public.
- **BuildBase or Stripe themselves:** those services have their own support.

> Issues, discussions and pull requests are public. Never post passwords, API keys, card or bank details, order numbers, or anyone's personal information, your own included.

How the rest works, and how issues are handled, is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
