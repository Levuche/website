# Security policy

Levuche is a product managed by Jagodana LLC ([jagodana.com](https://jagodana.com)). We take the security of Levuche and of the creators who use it seriously, and we welcome reports from anyone.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through GitHub:

1. Open the **Security** tab of this repository.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Describe the problem and send the report.

Only the Levuche maintainers can see a private report. Please don't open a public issue, pull request or discussion about a vulnerability, and don't share it elsewhere until we've had a chance to fix it.

## What to include

- What the problem is and where it happens (a repository, a page on levuche.com or app.levuche.com, or a creator site on levuche.page)
- Steps to reproduce it
- What an attacker could do with it
- Any proof of concept, screenshots or logs that help

## What happens next

We'll acknowledge your report in the private advisory, keep you updated there while we look into it, and tell you when it's fixed. If you'd like, we'll credit you in the published advisory.

## Scope

This policy covers Levuche's own repositories, levuche.com, app.levuche.com and creator sites on levuche.page.

Levuche uses BuildBase as a third-party service for products, checkout, files and some account screens. Problems in BuildBase itself should go to BuildBase. If you're not sure which side a problem belongs to, report it here and we'll pass it on.

## Good faith

We won't take action against anyone who reports in good faith, stays within this policy, avoids accessing or changing other people's data, and gives us reasonable time to fix the problem before talking about it publicly.
